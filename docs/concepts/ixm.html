IXM (Read: Eye Exam)

Problem

Eye diseases like open-angle glaucoma are often painless and do not have clear symptoms in their early stages. As a result, they may go undiagnosed until it becomes too late.

Furthermore, traditional eye tests tend to be labor-intensive and sometimes prone to human error.

Buildiful Solution

IXM is a deep learning system that can analyze retinal images and report signs of abnormality.

Its goal is to catch eye diseases and/or their related health issues early on.

It is also important to note that IXM is intended to be a complementary tool for doctors, as opposed to their replacement.
