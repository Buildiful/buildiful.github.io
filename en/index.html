Latest Mission

Seeing Eye Robots and Technologies

A multi-tier initiative to empower the visually impaired to thrive in the digital era.

Tier 1 - Braille Literacy Enhancements


Braille Buildies:


Open-source, 3D-printed bricks for both sighted and visually impaired groups to learn Braille at their own pace.



Interactive App:


An equally fun way to improve your Braille knowledge on the go.





* * *



🏆 Newsflash:


Our Braille lookup app has just won a Product Hunt Makers Festival award!


Tier 2 - Seeing Eye Interfaces and Robots


To start off with lower entry barriers, we are building seeing eye interfaces for existing consumer robots.

Then we will gauge where to go from there.




Tier 3 - Sightless Computer Code


This is an exceedingly exciting part:

We are building not just a sightless code infrastructure, but also an ecosystem where anyone can learn to code.

A good product vision is required; a 20/20 vision is not.
