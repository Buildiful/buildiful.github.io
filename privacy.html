Privacy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

A simple catalog

Buildiful 365 is a static public homepage featuring free tools. You do not need an account to browse the catalog. Searching, filtering, and sorting operate within your browser. We do not deliberately send those search terms to an analytics provider. The homepage does not upload personal files. Individual tools have their own behavior and privacy information.

Basic website analytics

On the official Buildiful GitHub Pages site, Google Analytics 4 (GA4) may count page visits and the catalog's tool-opening or source-code-opening actions. Tool events contain only an allowlisted event name and a slug taken from the published catalog, not what you type into the search box. The site configures analytics without advertising consent, Google Signals, or ad personalization.

When enabled, GA4 may use first-party cookies, pseudonymous identifiers, and browser or network information from which approximate location can be derived. Page URLs sent by this module exclude query strings and fragments. See Google's Privacy Policy and GA4 cookie information.

Regional privacy choices

Where consent is required for this analytics configuration, you can allow or decline before GA4 loads. In other eligible regions, basic analytics may start without an extra banner. Where an objection option is applicable, it is offered. Supported Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track browser signals disable analytics. If the regional check fails or returns an unknown region, GA4 remains off until you give permission.

Regional check and saved choices

To select a regional privacy experience, this site may request a coarse location classification from IPWhois. The provider can receive your network IP address from that request. Only a coarse consent category is cached in browser storage for up to 12 hours; this module does not save the full lookup response, IP address, or coordinates.

If you explicitly allow or decline analytics, that choice is saved in local storage under buildiful.analytics.v2.choice for up to 365 days. Tools hosted on the same origin may recognize that choice. A previous explicit decision remains changeable from this page as appropriate. Withdrawing permission stops subsequent GA tracking by this module; data previously processed by Google is not automatically deleted, and existing cookies may remain until cleared or expired. GA4 data retention depends on its property settings.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages hosts the homepage and may process ordinary webpage requests independently of Google Analytics. Links to individual tools, their source repositories, Google, and Buildiful.com lead to services with their own privacy practices. For privacy questions or applicable data rights requests, use the contact details at Buildiful.com.
