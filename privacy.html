Privacy

Updated November 18, 2026

A simple catalog

Buildiful 365 is a static public homepage that helps you discover free tools. You do not need an account to browse or open them. This site does not ask for or upload your files. Each linked tool has its own privacy information and local storage behavior.

Optional Google Analytics

We ask permission before loading Google Analytics 4 (GA4). If you accept, GA4 receives limited usage information, including page visits and which tool or source-code links are opened. Google may process cookies, approximate location, browser/device details, and other technical information. We configure Google Signals and advertising personalization to be disabled.

We do not intentionally send search terms typed into the catalog, private files, project content, or other user-provided text. Analytics page URLs exclude search queries and URL fragments. The site does not include advertising or tracking pixels outside optional GA4.

Your choice

If you decline or do not respond, the GA4 script is not loaded. Your consent decision and time are stored in your browser's local storage under buildiful.analytics.consent.v1. Other Buildiful tools on the same web origin may use that same setting. Choose “Analytics settings” on the homepage to change your preference. If you withdraw consent after analytics has loaded, the page reloads to stop further collection; already collected data is not automatically deleted from Google's systems. Analytics is disabled if you view a local file copy of the homepage.

External links

Opening one of our tool apps takes you to its own public webpage, and opening source code takes you to GitHub. Their separate terms and privacy policies may apply. Read Google's Privacy Policy for details about Google Analytics. For inquiries, use the public contact options at Buildiful.com.
